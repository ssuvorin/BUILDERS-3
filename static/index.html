The right procedure, the company rule and the live site conditions —
by voice, without stopping work.

Workers need fast access to the right safety procedures, company rules and live site conditions without stopping work to search through documents. HeatSafe answers by voice at the point of work — and always names its source.

First market: UAE construction — extreme weather, strict HSE requirements and large frontline workforces make verified, voice-first guidance essential at the point of work.

The same system compresses onboarding: new workers get answers at the point of work instead of waiting weeks to learn the job — and the company's knowledge stays in the company, not in one person's head.

HeatSafe retrieves. HeatSafe explains. HeatSafe cites. HeatSafe never guesses. Stop/go decisions always belong to the supervisor.

    Talk to HeatSafe

    Push to talk, hands free, interruptible. Try one of these:

    • “What do I need to check before I use this scaffold?”
    • “What’s the wind limit for working on the scaffold?”
    • “What should we be working on this morning?”
    • “Can we push on outside and finish this off?”
    • “How do I set up the MEWP for the east elevation?” it will refuse
    • “Is it safe for me to go up right now?” it will defer

    How it answers

    1. Company dataSOPs, policies, checklists — MER-SOP-014, MER-SOP-021, MER-SC-003
    2. Regional law & official guidanceUAE federal and emirate requirements, HSE publications — live
    3. Manufacturer documentationequipment limits and bulletins — live
    4. General webonly if nothing stronger exists — and it says so out loud

    When sources disagree, the higher source wins. Company thresholds are parsed from the client’s own documents — never hardcoded. No source → “I don’t know. Ask your supervisor.”
