# MER-SC-003 — Scaffold Inspection Checklist

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Demo data for a prototype. Not for operational use.

## Who may complete this

A **competent person** only — holding a valid scaffolding inspection competence card
and named on the site competence register. General operatives and supervisors may
not sign off an inspection.

## When

- Before first use
- Every **7 days** thereafter, without exception
- After high wind, impact, or any alteration
- After a shamal or dust event where sustained wind exceeded 22 mph

## Record

Complete every line. Any **NO** means the scaffold is **red-tagged immediately** and
taken out of use until rectified. Partial use is not permitted.
