# Meridian Construction — Working at Height SOP

Document: MC-SOP-021 | Revision 5 | Owner: HSE Manager

## Scope

Applies to all Meridian employees and subcontractors carrying out any task
where a person could fall a distance liable to cause injury: scaffolds,
mobile towers, MEWPs, ladders and roof work.

## Before you start (every shift)

1. Check you hold a current work-at-height briefing for this site.
2. Check the weather board or ask the assistant for current wind speed —
   limits are in the Wind & Weather Working Policy (MC-POL-014).
3. Confirm the scaffold displays a green Scafftag dated within the last 7 days.
4. Visually check your harness: webbing, stitching, buckles, lanyard,
   karabiner. Any cut, fraying or chemical staining → quarantine the harness
   and report it. Do not use it.
5. Clip your harness to a designated anchor point, never to guardrails
   or scaffold ties.

## Ladder use

- Ladders are for access and short-duration light work (under 30 minutes) only.
- Angle: 1 out for every 4 up (75 degrees).
- Three points of contact at all times; do not carry materials in your hands
  while climbing — use a tool belt or hoist line.
- Ladder must be tied at the top or footed by a second person.

## Mobile tower scaffolds

1. Build and alter towers only if you are PASMA-trained.
2. Use the 3T (through-the-trap) method when fitting guardrails.
3. Never move a tower with people or materials on the platform.
4. Lock all castors before anyone climbs.

## MEWP (cherry picker / scissor lift)

- Operators must hold a current IPAF licence for the machine category.
- Harness with a short restraint lanyard is mandatory in boom-type MEWPs,
  clipped to the manufacturer's anchor point in the basket.
- Ground condition check before setup: no cellars, drains or soft ground
  under outriggers without spreader plates.

## Dropped-object prevention

- Tether all hand tools above 2 m.
- No loose materials on platform edges; use brick guards and toe boards.
- Exclusion zone below any work at height, marked with barriers.

## If something goes wrong

- Rescue plan: every work-at-height task must have a named rescue person
  on site. Suspended worker → call the rescue person and 999, do not
  attempt an improvised rope rescue.
- Report all dropped objects and near misses to the site supervisor the
  same day.
