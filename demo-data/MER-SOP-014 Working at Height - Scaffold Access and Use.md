# MER-SOP-014 — Working at Height: Scaffold Access and Use

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Demo data for a prototype. Not for operational use.

## 1. Purpose

To set out how Meridian operatives access, use and work from scaffold structures,
and the checks required before doing so.

## 2. Before you go up — the four checks

Every operative, every time, before accessing a scaffold:

1. **Scafftag.** Check the tag at the access point. A **green** tag means the
   scaffold has been inspected and is fit for use. A **red** tag means do not
   access, under any circumstances. **No tag is the same as a red tag.**
2. **Date.** Confirm the inspection date on the tag is within the last **7 days**.
   Out of date means do not use — report it to your supervisor.
3. **Conditions.** Check current wind and temperature against **MER-SOP-021**. If
   you are unsure of the current reading, ask your supervisor before ascending.
4. **Visual.** Look for missing boards, missing guardrails, damaged or removed ties,
   and materials left on the platform from the previous shift.

If any of the four fails: **do not go up.** Report it.

## 3. Access

- Use the designated ladder access or stair tower only. **Never climb the frame.**
- Maintain three points of contact on ladders at all times.
- Ladders must extend at least 1 m above the platform and be secured at the top.
- One person on a ladder at a time.
- Tools and materials are raised by hoist or hand line — **never carried up a
  ladder.**

## 4. Working on the platform

- Guardrails, mid-rails and toe boards must be in place on all open edges. If any
  are missing, stop and report — do not improvise.
- **Never remove a guardrail, tie or board** to make a job easier. Only a qualified
  scaffolder may alter a scaffold (see section 6).
- Platforms must be fully boarded with no gaps greater than 25 mm.
- Maximum platform loading is stated on the Scafftag. If it isn't stated, assume
  light duty (0.75 kN/m²) and check with the supervisor.
- Keep the platform clear. Materials must not obstruct the walkway or be stacked
  above guardrail height.
- Tools must be tethered when working above other operatives or a public area.
- Exclusion zone below must be established and barriered before any work at height
  begins.

## 5. Harnesses and fall arrest

- A harness is required for any work where guardrail protection is incomplete, on
  any leading edge, and for all work from a MEWP.
- Inspect the harness before each use: webbing for cuts, fraying, chemical damage;
  stitching intact; buckles and D-rings undamaged; inspection tag in date.
- **Any harness involved in a fall arrest is removed from service permanently.** Do
  not reuse it. Tag it and hand it to the supervisor.
- Anchor points must be rated and approved — attach above shoulder height where
  possible. Never anchor to a guardrail, tie or handrail.
- Never work alone in a harness. A rescue plan must be in place before work begins —
  suspension trauma can be fatal within minutes.

## 6. Alterations — scaffolders only

Only operatives holding a valid **scaffolding competence card** may erect, alter,
add to or dismantle any part of a scaffold. This includes moving a single board or
removing a single tie.

If the scaffold does not suit the task, **stop and raise it with the supervisor.**
Do not adapt it yourself. Unauthorised alteration is a disciplinary matter and is
the single most common cause of scaffold collapse.

## 7. Inspection regime

- Full inspection by a competent person **before first use**, then at intervals
  **not exceeding 7 days.**
- Additional inspection after any event likely to have affected stability —
  high wind, impact from plant, or alteration.
- Results recorded on the Scafftag and in the site inspection register.
- Checklist: **MER-SC-003**.

## 8. Stopping work

Work at height stops immediately if:

- Wind or temperature reaches the suspension thresholds in **MER-SOP-021**
- Visibility drops below 1,000 m
- Any structural defect is found
- The Scafftag is red, missing or out of date
- An operative reports feeling unwell, dizzy or unsteady

**Any operative may stop work at height at any time on safety grounds without
needing permission.** Report it afterwards; do not seek approval first.

## 9. Escalation

| Situation | Contact |
|---|---|
| Defect found on a scaffold | Site Supervisor — radio channel 2 |
| Scaffold needs altering | Scaffold Supervisor — Ext. 4106 |
| Tag missing or out of date | Site Supervisor, then HSE — Ext. 4110 |
| Fall, near miss, or dropped object | **Stop work. Site emergency 800-4400.** Report within the hour. |
