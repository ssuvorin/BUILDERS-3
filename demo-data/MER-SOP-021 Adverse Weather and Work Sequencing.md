# MER-SOP-021 — Adverse Weather and Work Sequencing

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Created as demo data for a prototype. Thresholds below are
> Team 21 company policy for demonstration purposes and are deliberately stricter
> than commonly cited industry guidance. Do not use operationally.

## 1. Purpose

To define the environmental conditions under which external work must stop at
Team 21 sites, and to set out the internal work sequence supervisors should move
crews onto when external work is suspended.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all operatives, subcontractors and supervisors at Team 21 sites in the
UAE. Where a client or principal contractor imposes a stricter limit, **the stricter
limit applies.**

## 3. Wind thresholds

Wind readings are taken from the site anemometer at the highest working level, or
from the nearest official meteorological station where no site reading is available.

| Condition | Sustained wind | Gusts | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal working | Below 17 mph (27 km/h) | Below 25 mph (40 km/h) | External work may proceed |
| Restricted | 17–22 mph (27–35 km/h) | 25–33 mph (40–53 km/h) | No work above 6 m. No sheeting, panel handling or material hoisting. Ground-level external work may continue. |
| Suspended | Above 22 mph (35 km/h) | Above 33 mph (53 km/h) | **All external work stops.** Scaffold to be cleared of loose materials. Crews move to internal sequence (section 7). |

**Note for supervisors:** Team 21's 17 mph limit for work above 6 m is deliberately
lower than the figure commonly cited in general industry guidance. Team 21 policy
takes precedence on Team 21 sites. If an operative quotes a higher figure from an
external source, the Team 21 limit stands.

Handling large sheet materials, formwork panels or anything acting as a sail:
**stop at 15 mph sustained**, regardless of working height.

## 4. Temperature and heat

| Condition | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Below 42 °C | Normal working, standard hydration breaks |
| Elevated | 42–45 °C | Mandatory 15-min shaded rest per hour. Buddy system for all external work. No lone working outdoors. |
| Suspended | Above 45 °C | All external work stops. Move to internal sequence. |

**Summer midday break.** Between **15 June and 15 September**, work in direct sun or
in open external areas is **prohibited between 12:30 and 15:00**. This is a
company-wide rule and is not subject to supervisor discretion. Crews move to the
internal sequence (section 7) or take the scheduled break.

Humidity: where relative humidity exceeds 60% and temperature exceeds 40 °C, treat
as the next band up.

Cold working: below 5 °C, no concrete pours without a warm-weather admixture
approved by the site engineer. (Rare at UAE sites; included for completeness.)

## 5. Dust, sand and shamal conditions

| Condition | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Reduced visibility | Below 1,000 m | No crane operations. No work above 6 m. |
| Severe | Below 500 m | All external work stops. Secure loose materials and sheeting. |

Shamal events frequently combine high wind with reduced visibility — **assess both,
and apply whichever is more restrictive.**

Respiratory protection (FFP2 minimum) is mandatory for any external work during a
declared dust event, including at ground level.

## 6. Rain

Rare, but when it occurs:

- No work on scaffold platforms without slip assessment by the supervisor.
- No electrical work in exposed external areas.
- Excavations to be checked for water ingress and side stability before re-entry.

## 7. Internal work sequence (when external work is suspended)

When external work is stopped, supervisors should redirect crews to the following,
in priority order:

1. **Internal blockwork and partitions** — floors 3–8, currently accessible
2. **First-fix M&E** — Tower A, levels 4–6
3. **Internal plastering and screed prep** — levels 2–3
4. **Material preparation and cutting** in the covered laydown area
5. **Toolbox talks, permit review and equipment inspection** — always available
6. **Housekeeping and waste segregation** in internal areas

Crews must not be left idle on site. If none of the above is available, the
supervisor escalates to the Site Manager.

## 8. Who decides

- **Supervisor** monitors conditions and applies the thresholds above.
- **Site Manager** authorises resumption of external work after a suspension.
- **HSE Manager** may suspend work at any time regardless of readings.

**No operative is required to work in conditions they consider unsafe, regardless of
what the readings say.** Raise it with your supervisor.

## 9. Escalation

| Situation | Contact |
|---|---|
| Conditions approaching a threshold | Site Supervisor — radio channel 2 |
| Resumption after suspension | Site Manager — Ext. 4102 |
| Disagreement over a reading | HSE Manager — Ext. 4110 |
| Immediate danger | **Stop work. Site emergency number 800-4400.** |
