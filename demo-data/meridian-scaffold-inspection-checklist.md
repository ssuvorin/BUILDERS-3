# Meridian Construction LLC — Scaffold Inspection Checklist

Document: MC-CHK-007 | Revision 2 | Owner: Site Manager | Sites: all UAE sites

## When to inspect

- Before first use after erection or alteration.
- Every 7 days while the scaffold remains in place (Meridian standard;
  aligned with UAE regulatory requirements).
- After any event likely to affect stability: high winds above the
  MC-POL-014 limits, impact by plant, heavy rain, sandstorm.
- Inspections are carried out by a competent person holding a current
  third-party scaffold inspector certification accepted in the UAE.

## Checklist

### Base and foundations

- [ ] Standards vertical, on sole boards and base plates, no sinking or wash-out.
- [ ] No standing water or excavation within 2 m of the scaffold base.

### Structure

- [ ] All ledgers and transoms in place, couplers tight.
- [ ] Bracing complete as per the approved scaffold design drawings.
- [ ] Ties present and secure at the spacing shown on the handover certificate;
      no ties removed without a scaffolder's sign-off.

### Platforms

- [ ] Boards free of cracks, excessive knots, oil, and accumulated sand.
- [ ] No gaps over 25 mm between boards; boards do not overhang more than
      4x their thickness.
- [ ] Guardrails: top rail at minimum 950 mm, intermediate rail so no gap
      exceeds 470 mm, toe boards at least 150 mm on every working platform.
- [ ] Brick guards fitted where materials are stacked above toe-board height.

### Access

- [ ] Ladder access secured, extends at least 1 m above the platform.
- [ ] Ladder gates or trap doors close automatically behind the user.

### Signage and tagging

- [ ] Scafftag holder at every access point; green tag dated and signed.
- [ ] Incomplete scaffold sections fenced off and fitted with a red
      "Do not use" tag.

## Recording

Record every inspection in the site scaffold register within 24 hours.
A copy of the register page goes to the HSE Manager weekly. Defects found →
red-tag the affected section immediately and inform the site supervisor
before any remedial work starts.
