# MER-SOP-008 — Hand and Power Tool Inspection and Tagging

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Demo data for a prototype. Not for operational use.

## 1. Purpose

To set out the routine inspection and colour-tagging of hand tools and portable power
tools at Team 21 sites.

This is a **routine housekeeping task**, not a safety-critical procedure. It is one of
the standing internal jobs crews move onto when external work is stopped
(**MER-SOP-021 §7**).

## 2. Who may do this

**Any trained operative.** No competence card is required. If you are unsure about a
tool, set it aside and ask — you do not need to make a judgement call.

## 3. When

- **Before first use** of any tool new to site
- **Every quarter**, on the colour-code cycle below
- **After any drop, impact or visible damage**
- **On return** from an external hire

## 4. Colour codes

| Quarter | Months | Tag colour |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | January – March | Red |
| Q2 | April – June | Green |
| Q3 | July – September | **Blue** |
| Q4 | October – December | Yellow |

A tool carrying the **current quarter's colour** is in date. Any other colour, or no
tag at all, means it has not been inspected this quarter — set it aside for
inspection. Do not use it.

Tags are held in the site store. Sign out the roll, sign it back in.

## 5. The inspection — six checks

Work through these in order for each tool.

1. **Body.** No cracks, no missing screws, casing closed and intact.
2. **Cable and plug** (power tools). Run the full length of the cable through your
   hand. Look for cuts, nicks, taped repairs, exposed conductor, and cable pulling out
   of the plug or the tool body. Check the plug for cracks and bent or burnt pins.
3. **Guards.** Present, secure, and moving freely if they are meant to move. Never
   removed or tied back.
4. **Switch.** Operates cleanly, returns to off, does not stick. Where fitted, the
   dead-man switch releases properly.
5. **Working parts.** Blades, bits, discs and heads secure and undamaged. Handles
   tight, heads secure, no split or taped shafts.
6. **Chuck, keys and accessories.** Present and undamaged. Chuck keys attached where
   they belong.

## 6. Recording the result

**Pass** — every check clear:

1. Remove the old tag.
2. Fit the current quarter's tag.
3. Write the date and your initials on the tag.
4. Log it on the tool register in the site store.

**Fail** — any check not clear:

1. Fit a **red DO NOT USE tag**.
2. Remove the tool from the work area to the **quarantine box** in the site store.
3. Log the defect on the register — write what is wrong, not just "faulty".
4. Tell the storeman so it can be replaced or sent for repair.

A failed tool goes in the quarantine box the same shift. Do not leave it on the bench
with a red tag and no one watching it.

## 7. Do not repair anything

This procedure is inspection only. **Do not repair a tool, and do not tape a cable.**
A taped cable is an automatic fail, not a fix. Faulty tools go to the storeman.

Portable appliance testing is a separate job carried out by the site electrician. This
inspection is **visual only** — it does not replace a PAT test and does not certify a
tool electrically.

## 8. Escalation

| Situation | Contact |
|---|---|
| Unsure whether a tool passes | Storeman — site store |
| Tool needs repair or replacement | Storeman — site store |
| Damaged tool found in use | Site Supervisor — radio channel 2 |
| Burning smell, sparking or shock from a tool | **Stop. Unplug at the socket. Site Supervisor immediately, channel 2.** |
